Before a lift or carry after closing a gripper, inspect the close result. If holding is False, or the reported width is inconsistent with the intended object, do not lift or travel as though a grasp succeeded. Open, retreat vertically if needed, and re-observe/re-localize before another approach. A non-empty close alone does not prove that the intended object is held.

When a motion is explicitly refused by the IK/reach solver, do not immediately resend the same pose with the same arm and unchanged parameters. Inspect the returned reason. Use a suggested reachable alternative, change the approach or arm, or re-observe before making a different attempt. Preserve the original task's required order and completion conditions.
